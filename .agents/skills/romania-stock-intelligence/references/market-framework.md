# Coverage and research workflow

## 1. Romanian equities and market structure
- BET: price index. BET-TR: dividend-inclusive total return. Do not compare percentage returns between different methodologies as if they were the same instrument.
- Capture current official BET constituents/weights; `TLV`, `SNP`, `SNG`, `H2O`, `BRD`, `EL` are examples of issuers to verify, not a permanent constituent list.
- Breadth = verified number of advancers, decliners and unchanged *within declared universe*; explicitly say whether all BVB names or only BET constituents.
- Contribution = index return attributable to constituents requires reliable constituent weights, dates and adjusted component return methodology. Without necessary inputs, say `无法确认权重股贡献` rather than crude attribution.
- Breadth-vs-concentration: use (a) advancers share of universe, (b) official weights of leaders, (c) share of signed index contribution where calculable, (d) total turnover and liquidity. Distinguish broad breadth from a few heavyweights lifting the headline index.
- Turnover vs volume: RON traded value is not number of shares; auction/session aggregation and cross-market trades need consistent scope.
- Industry: financials/banks, oil/gas producers, gas infrastructure, electricity/hydropower, utilities, transport/consumer/telecom where actual listed firms support analysis.
- Dividend: announce ex-date, record date, payment date separately; corporate actions cause apparent price jumps that may not be underlying performance.
- Earnings: revenue, net profit, margins, net debt/cash, cash flow, prior period, inflation-adjusted dynamics, guidance, one-offs, earnings surprise vs **sourced** consensus only.

## 2. Forex, energy, yields and spillover
- EUR/RON: label direction precisely; **RON per EUR rises = RON depreciates against EUR**. BNR/ECB reference fixes are not live tradable FX quotes.
- EUR/USD: USD per EUR rises = EUR strengthens vs USD. Do not invert units silently.
- Brent: contract/delivery month and USD/barrel; contrast supplier revenue sensitivity with importer/consumer cost effects, taxes and local regulated prices.
- European gas: specify hub (e.g. Dutch TTF), delivery contract, settlement/spot, EUR/MWh or actual unit; consider Romanian domestic production and regulation, not simple 1:1 impact.
- Rates: Romania/EU/U.S. 2Y/10Y where verifiable, sovereign spread (`Romania 10Y - Germany 10Y` both same date and yield basis); rate change in **basis points**, not %. Bonds generally have inverse price/yield relationship; actual impact conditioned by duration and cash flow.
- European/U.S. indices: separate close time and calendar trading day, including market holidays; label sessions and currency. Discuss U.S. tech/AI strength as possible investor-sentiment backdrop, not direct proof for BET movement.
- Macro: Romanian HICP/CPI, GDP, deficit, public debt, fiscal changes, BNR decisions, ECB/Fed policy, ratings, budgets, political or regulatory developments using only dated verified documents.

## 3. Daily questions
1. Is the BVB open, closed, or yet to open? Have verified final prints arrived?
2. What changed vs **prior local trading close**? What changed vs **most recent delivered briefing**? Keep separate.
3. Was BET driven by concentration in financial/energy mega-weights or broad sector participation?
4. What moves from EUR/RON, EUR/USD, Brent, European gas, yields and external equities are potentially material to Romania?
5. Which announcements are new, changed, or upcoming? What are publication and effective times?
6. What are the top 3–5 themes, plausible transmission mechanisms and counterevidence?
7. What would falsify the leading interpretation?

## 4. Analysis sequencing
**Data observation** → **comparison** → **plausible mechanism** → **Romanian sector/issuer link** → **falsification / scenario**. Tag each section `事实`, `分析`, `判断/条件`. Never present a plausible mechanism as a proven causal effect.

## 5. Controls
- No price target or buy/sell order unless specifically requested for a reasoned non-personal analysis; even then explain assumptions, risks and uncertainty.
- No 'everyone is making money' claims; no manufactured member messages/testimonials.
- No charts with dummy financial indicator values. A missing panel is better than fabricated visual numbers.
